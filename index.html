Things to try

  1. Press on a song, or PLAY in the bottom bar, to start a song. Menu → Show has options for how PLAY and STOP behave.
  2. Tap a song's to open its sections, then on a section to jump there.
  3. Tap on a section to loop it. LOOP OFF lets the song carry on.
  4. Watch the endings: plays on into the next song, stops. Tap one to switch it.
  5. Menu → Plan: drag songs into a new order, add a placeholder, and save.
  6. Some songs have notes on the Live timeline for lighting, sound and other techs. See them on the controller: Menu → View → Show crew notes.
  7. at the top switches to Focus view. finds any song.
  8. In Focus view, tap the progress bar or to show the song's sections.
  9. With playback stopped, tap a section for its launch options: start right away, or pre-roll 1 or 2 bars before it.
  10. The Viewer follows everything, and has its own controls for that device.
  11. Look through the other options and settings in the menu.

On the Mac, Showpathy also has keyboard and MIDI pedal control, the Max for Live companion, backups, and Remote viewers for your crew anywhere.
